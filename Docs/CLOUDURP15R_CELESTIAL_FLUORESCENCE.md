# CLOUDURP15R – Celestial Fluorescence

## Summary
CLOUDURP15R exaggerates the visible Sun/Moon presentation stylistically without enabling a general scene bloom pass.

## Changes
- rendered solar disc enlarged to roughly 3× the earlier apparent radius;
- Sun receives a strong three-lobe local fluorescent bloom;
- Moon receives a much stronger silvery local bloom and slightly whiter, less chromatic surface presentation;
- bloom remains celestial-local rather than full-scene, preserving crisp UI/scene rendering.

## Intent
Match the user's request for Sun and Moon that read as bright fluorescent bodies in the sky rather than restrained crisp textures.
