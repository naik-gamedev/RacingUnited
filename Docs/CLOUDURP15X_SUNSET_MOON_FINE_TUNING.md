# CLOUDURP15X – Sunset and Moon Fine Tuning

## Summary
A celestial tuning pass after CLOUDURP15W.

## Changes
- successful Sun size/halo balance from 15W retained;
- low-angle solar disc tint pushed much further toward orange/red;
- Moon inner halo made smaller;
- Moon outer glow made 3x larger in reach but only 1/4 of the prior intensity;
