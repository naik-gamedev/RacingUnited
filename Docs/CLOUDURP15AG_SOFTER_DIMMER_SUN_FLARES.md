# CLOUDURP15AG – Softer, Dimmer Sun Flares

## Summary
A small tuning pass after CLOUDURP15AF focused only on the Sun flare starburst.

## Changes
- solar flare intensity reduced to roughly one tenth of the previous level;
- flare spikes softened substantially;
- flare envelope made gentler/less harsh;
- sun halo and screen-space object-occluded shaft logic retained.

## Intent
Match the user's request for Sun flares that are softer and much less intense.
