# CLOUDURP15U – Sunset Sun and Moon Tuning

## Summary
A tuning pass for celestial presentation after CLOUDURP15T.

## Changes
- Sun keeps very strong local bloom, but regains a faint readable body shape;
- low-angle solar tint is pushed much more strongly toward red/orange at sunset and sunrise;
- Moon stays white/luminous, but its inner halo is slightly smaller;
- Moon outer halo is reduced to roughly half of the previous presence;
- Moon scene illumination is reduced to 80 percent of the previous level.

## Intent
Better match the user's sunset reference and preferred Moon halo/scene-light balance.
