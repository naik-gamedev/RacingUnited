# CLOUDURP15J — astronomical day/night + moon

- cloud shapes reverted toward the earlier trusted look
- new from-scratch astronomical environment core replaces the previous simple sinusoid
- sky horizon, zenith and twilight are derived from solar elevation
- moon texture from `Assets/Scenes/Moon.png` is rendered in the visible sky
- moon phase progresses over a synodic cycle and moonlight can illuminate the scene slightly at night
