# CLOUDURP15AB – Moon Outer Halo Dimmer

## Summary
A final Moon-only tuning pass after CLOUDURP15AA.

## Changes
- Moon outer halo intensity halved relative to 15AA;
- Moon outer halo size/profile retained;
- Moon scene illumination reduced to 60 percent of the previous level.

## Intent
Reduce how much the nighttime atmosphere and scene feel lit by the Moon while preserving the overall Moon shape/halo balance.
