# CELESTIAL12 — Dark Rural Night Sky

CELESTIAL11 removed the global nocturnal haze veil, but the fallback night sky and
Moon-driven sky lift still left the dome too bright and too blue compared with a
real dark rural night. This pass darkens the baseline night sky substantially
while preserving the Moon disc, its local halo, and the continuous CELESTIAL10
night-to-dawn scattering transition.

## What changes

- Deep-night sky zenith/horizon fallback colours are reduced to near-black.
- Ground night fallback colours are darkened to match the sky calibration.
- The global Moon sky/ground lift is reduced sharply so that Moon presence no
  longer brightens the full dome unrealistically.
- Star visibility improves primarily through darker sky background rather than
  another large artificial star boost.

## What does not change

- CELESTIAL10 dawn/dusk scatter continuity.
- CELESTIAL11 zero global night air-light policy.
- Local Moon halo rendering and Moon directional lighting.
- Future localized mist around headlights/streetlights remains a separate
  low-altitude local-light scattering problem.
