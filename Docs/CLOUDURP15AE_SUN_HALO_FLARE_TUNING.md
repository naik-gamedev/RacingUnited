# CLOUDURP15AE – Sun Halo and Flare Tuning

## Summary
Keeps the object-occluded screen-space sun shafts, but retunes the visible Sun bloom and flare scale.

## Changes
- Sun halo radii reduced by about 20 percent;
- solar flare/starburst envelope reduced to roughly one third of its previous size;
- screen-space object-occluded shaft logic remains intact.

## Intent
Preserve the fact that geometry now carves the rays, while making the bright Sun presentation less oversized.
