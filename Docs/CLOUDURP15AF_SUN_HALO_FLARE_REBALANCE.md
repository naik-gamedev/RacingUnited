# CLOUDURP15AF – Sun Halo and Flare Rebalance

## Summary
A follow-up tuning pass after CLOUDURP15AE.

## Changes
- apparent Sun halo footprint reduced to roughly one third of the 15AE size;
- solar flare/starburst footprint increased to twice the 15AE size;
- screen-space object-occluded shaft logic remains intact.

## Intent
Match the user's latest preference: a much tighter Sun bloom with a larger starburst flare.
