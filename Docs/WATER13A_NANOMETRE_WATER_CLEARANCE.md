# WATER13A – Nanometre Water Clearance Test

The minimum presentation-only geometric clearance of the adaptive 3D water skin is changed from 3 mm to 0.001 micrometre (1 nanometre, 1e-9 m), exactly as requested.

The adaptive tessellation algorithm, water simulation, visible-depth thresholds, and reversed-Z polygon offset are otherwise unchanged so this test isolates the geometric clearance variable.
