# CLOUDURP15S – Solar Tint and Moon Halo Tuning

## Summary
Tuning pass on the stylised celestial presentation after CLOUDURP15R.

## Changes
- visible Sun size reduced from ~3x the prior apparent radius to ~2x;
- low-sun solar disc tint pushed harder toward orange/red at sunset and sunrise;
- Sun retains strong local bloom, but with a slightly more controlled body size;
- Moon keeps broad fluorescent bloom, but the tight inner halo is reduced.

## Intent
Keep the dramatic celestial look while better matching the user's sunset reference and preferred Moon halo balance.
