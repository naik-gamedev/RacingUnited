# CLOUDURP15V – Sun Disc and Moon Halo Tuning

## Summary
A refinement pass after CLOUDURP15U.

## Changes
- Sun keeps strong local bloom, but the disc/body is made more readable;
- the Sun becomes progressively more readable as it approaches sunset;
- low-angle solar colour remains strongly driven toward red/orange;
- Moon outer halo power is halved again;
- Moon scene illumination remains at the reduced 0.40 scene scale.

## Intent
Match the user's request for a still-glowy but more readable sunset Sun and a tighter Moon halo.
