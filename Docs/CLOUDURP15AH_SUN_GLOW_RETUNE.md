# CLOUDURP15AH – Sun Glow Retune

## Summary
A small solar tuning pass after CLOUDURP15AG.

## Changes
- main Sun halo/glow made stronger;
- halo footprint reduced to about 80 percent of the previous size;
- high-Sun glow shifted a little whiter;
- sunset/sunrise solar glow/disc pushed redder;
- softened Sun flares and object-occluded shafts retained.

## Intent
Match the user's request for a brighter, slightly whiter daytime solar glow with a tighter footprint and more clearly red sunset behaviour.
