HERITAGE ENGINE STEP 29J.4 - PLAYER SCENE TRIANGLE DRIVE SURFACE

1. Run Tools\BuildAndRunStep29J4.cmd.
2. Open the prototype scene and SCENE > WORLD.
3. Press LOAD / RELOAD PLAYER SCENE.
4. With the exact PlayerScene.obj / PlayerScene_Collision.obj supplied while
   diagnosing this step, expected diagnostics are approximately:
      Drive-surface triangles: 7917
      Spawn source: marker
      Spawn X: ~0.000
      Spawn ground-derived vehicle Y: terrain near -0.860 m + chassis reset offset
      Spawn Z: ~-46.908 m
5. The Peugeot should spawn at the SPAWN_PLAYER road location, not on top of the
   whole terrain bounding box.
6. Drive. Suspension/tire raycasts should follow actual sloped terrain triangles.

KNOWN STEP-29J.4 LIMITATION:
- Triangle scene geometry currently participates in read-only physics queries
  (especially suspension/tire raycasts), not the full rigid-body contact solver.
  The chassis can therefore pass through arbitrary triangle walls/buildings.
  Full static-mesh/convex body collision is a later world-physics milestone.

  PlayerScene.obj, or PlayerScene_Collision.obj.
