HERITAGE ENGINE - STEP 29F.1 TEST CHECKLIST
============================================

1. Run Tools\BuildAndRunStep29F1.cmd.
2. Confirm the prototype scene loads without a Lua error.
3. Confirm the top of the panel has:
   VEHICLE | PHYSICS | ENTITY | MODULE | SCENE | SAFETY
4. Under VEHICLE confirm the smaller tabs exist:
   DRIVE | SURFACES | TIRES | DRIVETRAIN | ABS / TCS | TELEMETRY
5. Open VEHICLE -> SURFACES. The split-grip and surface-runway buttons should
   be immediately visible without searching a long scroll panel.
6. Click RESET ON SPLIT GRIP - LEFT ASPHALT / RIGHT ICE and confirm:
   FL/RL = asphalt and FR/RR = ice after the car settles.
7. Switch between VEHICLE -> SURFACES, PHYSICS, and ENTITY. Unrelated old
   debug primitives should hide automatically; the simulation itself stays alive.
8. SCENE provides manual CLEAN VEHICLE / SURFACE TEST / PHYSICS LAB /
   ENTITY-PREFAB / SHOW ALL presentation presets.
9. Confirm driving, steering, gears, ABS/TCS and handbrake still behave as Step 29F.
10. Optional: SAFETY -> RUN ENGINE SAFETY SMOKE TESTS.
