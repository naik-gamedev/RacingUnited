HERITAGE ENGINE STEP 29J.2 - QUICK CHECK

1. Run Tools\BuildAndRunStep29J2.cmd.
2. If Release x64 builds, open the PROTOTYPE scene.
3. Go to SCENE > WORLD.
4. Click LOAD / RELOAD PLAYER SCENE.
5. Expected:
   - old physics-lab clutter disappears;
   - a simple parking-lot-style Player Scene appears;
   - collision proxy count is greater than zero;
   - the car resets near the Player Scene origin and can drive on the floor;
   - outer wall/kerb proxy boxes stop the car.
6. Replace Assets\Scenes\Player\PlayerScene.obj with your own 1:1 Blender scene when ready.
7. For collision, replace PlayerScene_Collision.obj with simple axis-aligned proxy boxes.
8. Double-click any numeric slider to enter an exact keyboard value, then press Enter.
