HERITAGE ENGINE - STEP 29G TEST CHECKLIST
=========================================

1. Run Tools\BuildAndRunStep29G.cmd.
2. Confirm the prototype scene loads without a Lua error.
3. Open VEHICLE -> TIRES. Confirm the tire page has:
      BASIC | CURVE | LIVE
4. Drive normally on dry asphalt. Steering/acceleration should remain stable,
   but tire breakaway/recovery should feel more progressive than Step 29F.
5. Open TIRES -> LIVE. While accelerating in a straight line, observe the
   front-left pure/combined force telemetry.
6. Accelerate or brake while steering. "Combined scale" should sometimes fall
   below 1.000 as longitudinal and lateral forces compete for the same grip.
7. At larger slip angles, pneumatic trail should decrease rather than remaining
   fixed. Aligning torque should therefore rise and then fade with saturation.
8. Open VEHICLE -> SURFACES and repeat the split-grip test. FL/RL should still
   report asphalt while FR/RR report ice.
9. Confirm gears, reverse, ABS/TCS, handbrake, steering and Ackermann still work.
10. Optional: adjust CURVE parameters, then reset the car. The debug sliders
    should alter progressive breakaway without a Lua/API error.

If anything fails, send the first compiler/Lua error or a screenshot plus:
UserData\Diagnostics\step29g_build_and_run.txt
