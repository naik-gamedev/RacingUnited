Heritage Engine - Step 29J.3 Player Spawn / Collision Bridge

1. Extract the Step 29J.3 ZIP over the RacingUnited project.
2. Your PlayerScene.obj, PlayerScene_Collision.obj, PlayerCar.obj and PlayerWheel.obj are not supplied by this update and must remain yours.
3. Run Tools\BuildAndRunStep29J3.cmd.
4. Open the prototype -> SCENE -> WORLD -> LOAD / RELOAD PLAYER SCENE.
5. Existing scene without marker:
   - Spawn source should show "auto-ground" when a suitable road/ground proxy is found.
   - The car should reset onto that proxy instead of blindly at world origin.
6. Exact authored spawn (recommended):
   - In the COLLISION Blender scene add a small cube named SPAWN_PLAYER.
   - Put the cube physically on the road at the desired starting point.
   - Export PlayerScene_Collision.obj again and reload the Player Scene.
   - Spawn source should show "marker".
   - The marker does not become a collider.
7. The temporary OBJ bridge faces the vehicle along Blender +Y.
8. Remember: changing a Blender object's origin/pivot alone does not move OBJ vertex coordinates and therefore cannot define the spawn point.
9. Collision is still one axis-aligned box per OBJ object/group. Use simple collision proxy objects rather than a duplicate of a detailed final scene where possible.
