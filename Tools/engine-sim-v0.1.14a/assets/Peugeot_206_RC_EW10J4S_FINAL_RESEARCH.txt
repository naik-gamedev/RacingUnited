IMPORTANT SIMULATOR LIMITATION
------------------------------
Engine Simulator Community Edition does not reproduce the complete stock PSA
ECU, continuously controlled intake VVT, complete dual-mode intake system,
temperature-dependent idle controller, catalyst/muffler acoustics, or full
vehicle-body acoustic transmission.

Therefore some cam timing, flow tables, ignition timing, intake dimensions,
exhaust dimensions, friction, rotating masses, and combustion parameters in
the .mr are CALIBRATION PROXIES rather than claimed factory measurements.

POST-DYNO STALL NOTE
--------------------
The engine stopped after the final dyno run. This appears consistent with a
known Engine Simulator behavior/issue where an engine may fail to recover idle
after being run to the rev limit/dyno endpoint. This does not alter the measured
peak calibration. The Heritage audio-capture workflow should primarily use
steady RPM-hold captures below the limiter rather than repeated limiter sweeps.
